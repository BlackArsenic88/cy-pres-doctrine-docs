# Privacy Policy for Cy Pres Doctrine

**Effective Date:** September 27, 2026
**Last Updated:** September 27, 2026 — rewritten for the 2026-09-27 product pivot (see `cypres_sdlc.md`'s pivot note). The prior version of this policy described a farm-to-table discovery app plus an ingredient/equipment dropshipping marketplace; the marketplace module was shelved before any code was written, so this version removes every account/order/payment-related disclosure that described a feature that was never built and now isn't planned.

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer of Cy Pres Doctrine ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you download, install, or use our mobile application, **Cy Pres Doctrine** (the "Application" or "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By installing or using Cy Pres Doctrine, you agree to the terms of this policy. If you do not agree with these terms, please do not install or use the Application.

**The short version:** Cy Pres Doctrine helps you find well-regarded restaurants, farms, museums, parks, and other places near you. There's no account, no payments, and nothing to buy in the app. It uses your device's location (optional) to rank results by distance, and lets you save places to your own device. We do not sell your personal information, and we collect very little of it in the first place.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Location Data

With your consent, Cy Pres Doctrine accesses your device's location to rank nearby places by distance. Location access is **"When In Use"** only — the App does not track your location in the background, and does not access your location unless the App is open and you are using a location-based feature. You can decline or revoke location access at any time in your device's Settings; if you do, you can still use the app by entering a ZIP code or address instead.

### B. Search Input

A ZIP code or address you type is resolved into coordinates using free, keyless geocoding services (the U.S. Census Bureau's public geocoder for addresses, Zippopotam's public service for ZIP codes) — no advertising company or data broker receives it.

### C. Saved Places

Tapping the heart on a place saves it using on-device storage only. This data is never transmitted to us or to anyone else, is not tied to any account (there is no account), and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the App or clear its storage.

### D. Information We Do Not Collect

* We do not require or offer account creation, and collect no name, email, phone number, or physical address.
* We do not process payments, orders, or any commerce of any kind — the App has no such features.
* We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.
* We do not collect government identifiers (such as a Social Security Number or passport number).
* We do not use advertising SDKs, and we do not sell your personal information to data brokers or advertisers.

### E. Diagnostic Data

We may use standard crash-reporting and performance-diagnostic tools to identify and fix bugs (for example, during TestFlight or Google Play beta testing, and after release). This data is limited to technical information about app crashes and performance, and is not used to build an advertising profile of you. If we add any additional analytics or advertising SDK in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy first.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to:

* Show you nearby places, ranked by proximity to your location or a ZIP code/address you enter.
* Maintain, secure, and improve the App, including diagnosing and fixing bugs.

---

## 3. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information, and we have very little to share in the first place:

* **Mapping and geocoding services.** The App uses Apple Maps (iOS) or Google Maps (Android, since there is no Apple Maps SDK for Android) to render the map, and the U.S. Census Bureau's and Zippopotam's public geocoding services to resolve a ZIP code or address you type into coordinates. These queries use your coordinates or ZIP code only; no name, contact, or payment information exists to share, because none is collected.
* **Listing data sources.** To populate results, the App queries its own database, synced on a schedule from the [USDA Local Food Portal](https://www.usdalocalfoodportal.com) and a curated dataset of recognized restaurants, hotels, and attractions. These are one-way public data sources — your search doesn't reach USDA or the curated dataset's publisher.
* **Service providers.** We may share information with vendors who help us operate the App (for example, cloud hosting or crash-reporting), under contractual confidentiality and data-protection obligations, and only to the extent needed to provide their service to us.
* **Legal and safety.** We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Cy Pres Doctrine, our users, or the public.
* **Business transfers.** If Cy Pres Doctrine is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Privacy Policy or a successor policy of which you will be notified.

**"Sale" or "Share" under state privacy laws:** We do not sell personal information for money, and we do not share it with third parties for their own cross-context behavioral advertising.

## 4. Network Access

Cy Pres Doctrine requires an active internet connection for its core features. In the normal course of use, the App makes network requests to:

* our backend service, to search and display nearby places; and
* free geocoding services, to resolve a ZIP code or address you type into coordinates.

Saved places are stored on-device only and never leave your phone.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Cy Pres Doctrine is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us using the details in Section 10 and we will delete it.

## 6. Data Retention

* **Location and search input** are used at the moment of your search and are not retained beyond what's needed to return results.
* **Saved places** are stored only on your device for as long as the App remains installed, or until you remove them.
* We retain no account or order data, because none is collected.
