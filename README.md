# Cy Pres Doctrine

Cy Pres Doctrine is a privacy-first mobile application designed to help users discover exceptional places worth traveling to nearby. Listings—ranging from restaurants and farms to museums, parks, golf courses, and hotels—are dynamically ranked by distance from your current location or a custom ZIP code/address.

## Features

* **Location-Based Search:** Instantly discover places ranked by proximity to your current location or a specific ZIP code or address.
* **Curated Recognition:** Prominently displays recognition badges pulled from curated datasets, including Michelin stars, AAA Diamond ratings, and World's 50 Best placements.
* **USDA Local Food Data:** Integrates directly with the USDA Local Food Portal to surface local farmers' markets, CSAs, on-farm stores, and agritourism destinations.
* **Diverse Place Categories:** Easily browse restaurants, farms, museums, parks, golf courses, hotels, and more.
* **Zero Registration:** No account signup required—get immediate access to search results without sharing personal profiles.
* **On-Device Favorites:** Tap the heart icon to save favorite places locally on your device for offline access and privacy.
