## 1. What Cy Pres Doctrine Is

Cy Pres Doctrine is a discovery app. It helps you find well-regarded restaurants, farms, museums, parks, golf courses, hotels, and other places near you, ranked by distance, using data from the USDA Local Food Portal and a curated dataset of recognized places. **The App does not process orders, payments, reservations, or bookings of any kind** — it links out to a place's own website for anything beyond browsing.
